Why This Exists

Schools run on scattered, manual systems

📂

Data in Silos

Excel sheets, paper registers, and WhatsApp messages — each department has its own system that doesn't talk to any other.

⏱️

Administrative Overhead

Fee receipts, attendance sheets, TC generation, payroll calculations — all done manually, every month, consuming staff hours that should go to students.

📉

No Visibility

Management has no real-time view of curriculum quality, student performance, fee collection status, or staff attendance — decisions are made on stale data.

🔓

No Accountability

Without role-based access, there is no audit trail — who changed what, when, and why? Critical data can be edited or deleted by anyone with file access.

🤖

No AI Leverage

Teachers spend hours creating lesson content and assessments from scratch, when local AI tools (running on school hardware) could do the heavy lifting.

🌐

No Digital Presence

The school's public website is static or absent. Parents and prospective families have no way to engage with curriculum, events, or announcements online.

The Solution

One integrated platform. Built specifically for this school.

🏗️

Custom-built, not off-the-shelf

No license fees forever. Every feature fits the school's workflow exactly.

🔒

All data stays on school premises

AI inference runs on a school-owned server. Student data never leaves the campus network.

📈

Modular — grow at your pace

Start with curriculum. Add fee, payroll, assessment, website as needed. No big-bang deployment.

🧑‍💻

In-house team — full control

The school owns the code, the servers, and the team. No vendor dependency, no surprises.

Risk Management

Known risks and how we address them

Risk Likelihood Impact Mitigation
Key developer leaves mid-project Medium High Thorough code documentation, pair programming, no single point of knowledge
Timeline slippage on a phase Medium Medium Phased delivery — each phase is independently usable. Slippage delays next phase, not school operations.
AI server hardware failure Low Medium AI features degrade gracefully — rest of ERP continues. Warranty + maintenance contract on server.
Data loss / corruption Low High Daily automated backups to cloud, weekly off-site copy, PostgreSQL point-in-time recovery.
Staff resistance to adoption Medium Medium Phased rollout lets staff adapt gradually. In-house team provides direct support & training.
Scope creep inflating cost Medium Medium Phase-gated approvals — new features only considered after current phase is signed off by management.
